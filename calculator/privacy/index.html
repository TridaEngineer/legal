Calculator by Trida Media – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 16 November 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Android app Calculator (the "app"), published on Google Play by the developer Trida Media, operated by Trida ApS, Denmark ("we", "us").

Summary

The app does not collect, transmit or share any personal or sensitive user data. Nothing leaves your device. The app contains no advertising, no analytics and no telemetry.

Data the app collects

None. The app does not ask for an account, a name, an email address, your location, your contacts or any other personal information, and it sends no data to us or to anyone else.

Data kept on your device

All calculations are performed on your device. Any settings or calculation history the app keeps are stored only on your device and are never sent off it.

Sharing with third parties

We do not share any data with third parties, because we do not collect any. The app contains no advertising, analytics or telemetry.

Security

Because no data leaves your device, we hold no user data that could be exposed. Data kept on your device is stored in the app's private storage, which Android protects from other apps.

Data retention and deletion

We retain no user data. Data kept on your device remains there until you clear the app's data in Android settings or uninstall the app, which deletes it.

Google Play

The app is distributed through Google Play. Google may process information about your download and use of Google Play under its own terms, which we do not control. See the Google Privacy Policy.

Children

The app does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Since we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. If you have questions about your rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), contact us at the address below. You may also complain to the Danish Data Protection Agency (Datatilsynet).

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, we will update this page and its effective date before the change is released.
